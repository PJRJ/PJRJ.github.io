About

I am a doctoral researcher at the Interdisciplinary Centre for Security, Reliability and Trust (SnT) of the University of Luxembourg, where I am a member of the TruX research group. I work under the supervision of Prof. Jacques Klein, and my PhD is funded by the Luxembourg National Research Fund (FNR) through the CORE project UNLOCK.

My research focuses on Android app analysis at scale. I study what real-world apps actually contain and how they differ across distribution channels and regions, with the goal of making app-analysis research more reliable and exposing security and privacy risks that conventional analyses miss. I also contribute to AndroZoo, the largest publicly available collection of Android apps for research.

Research interests

  • Android app analysis
  • Mobile security & privacy
  • Mining software repositories
  • Large-scale empirical studies
  • App ecosystems & reproducibility

2026

MobiSys '26

GeoTwins: Uncovering Hidden Geographic Disparities in Android Apps

Marco Alecci, Pedro Jesús Ruiz Jiménez, Jordan Samhi, Tegawendé F. Bissyandé, Jacques Klein

Proceedings of the 24th Annual International Conference on Mobile Systems, Applications and Services (MobiSys), Cambridge, UK, pp. 83–95.

Shows that the same app can differ by country: GeoTwins share branding and functionality but ship under different package names with different permissions, third-party libraries and privacy disclosures, and even base APKs of App Bundles vary by region. Releases a dataset of 81,963 GeoTwins.

2025

SANER '25

Dissecting APKs from Google Play: Trends, Insights and Security Implications

Pedro Jesús Ruiz Jiménez, Jordan Samhi, Tegawendé F. Bissyandé, Jacques Klein

2025 IEEE International Conference on Software Analysis, Evolution and Reengineering (SANER), Montréal, Canada, pp. 728–739.

The first large-scale study of everything inside Android APKs, not just the bytecode and manifest. Across 400,000+ Google Play apps we find over 15,000 file extensions and 1,000+ file types, and show that disguised files (e.g. a DEX file named .png) are used to load malicious code at runtime.

MSR '24

AndroZoo: A Retrospective with a Glimpse into the Future

Marco Alecci, Pedro Jesús Ruiz Jiménez, Kevin Allix, Tegawendé F. Bissyandé, Jacques Klein

2024 IEEE/ACM 21st International Conference on Mining Software Repositories (MSR), Lisbon, Portugal, pp. 389–393.

Looks back on eight years of AndroZoo, the largest Android APK dataset available to researchers (~24 million APKs at the time, ~500,000 downloads per day), and introduces its Google Play metadata.
